# Additional opening video prompts

Use image-to-video, one continuous shot per generation, widescreen 16:9, approximately 5 seconds. Upload the named reference as the starting frame. Music and the logo impact are added in the game.

## 1. Racers charging towards camera

Reference: `buggy_only.png` (existing artwork).

Low front-facing tracking shot. The coral buggy leads the green and blue buggies charging towards the camera along the wooden tabletop. The camera tracks backwards just ahead of them as their tyres spin, suspension bounces and dust trails behind. The coral buggy gains on the camera, filling more of the frame at the end. Preserve the reference's drivers, vehicle shapes, colours, pixel-edged illustrated arcade style and tiny scale beneath oversized household objects. Drivers remain seated with hands on steering wheels. One continuous shot, no cuts, no text overlays, no logo.

## 2. Tabletop world reveal

Reference: `tabletop_wide_reference.png` (new matching artwork).

A slow elevated camera glide backwards and gently upwards reveals more of the miniature racing circuit on the sunlit wooden desk. The coral buggy and blue buggy follow the existing bend while oversized alphabet blocks, books, dice, a ruler bridge and a blue mug establish their tiny scale. Keep the track and household objects fixed in place. Preserve the reference's vehicle designs, drivers, warm lighting and pixel-edged illustrated arcade style. Smooth restrained motion, one continuous shot, no cuts, no text overlays, no logo.

## 3. Slow-motion stunt and landing

Reference: `jump.png` (existing artwork; vehicles begin airborne).

Continue the airborne jump shown in the reference. The coral buggy and lime monster truck travel forward in a brief slow-motion arc, with their wheels spinning and suspension extended. The camera tracks smoothly alongside them as they descend onto the clear tabletop beyond the book ramp. Tyres contact the wood, suspension compresses once and rebounds, dust bursts behind them, and both accelerate away. Preserve the reference's drivers, distinct vehicle designs, colours, miniature scale and pixel-edged illustrated arcade style. One continuous shot, no cuts, no text overlays, no logo.

Optional negative prompt for all three: vehicle morphing, changing colours, extra wheels, missing wheels, duplicate drivers, standing drivers, floating props, full-size cars, photorealism, scene changes, captions, logos, watermarks.

Suggested edit order: world reveal early, stunt during the build, charging racers just before the logo impact around 45 seconds. The drop timing belongs to the edit, not the generation prompt.
