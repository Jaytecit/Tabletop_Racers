# Breakfast asset provenance

All breakfast geometry, labels and surface shaders are original procedural work authored for this project in scripts/tracks/kitchen_art.gd and scripts/tracks/author_plate_rim.gd. Plate Rim adds an original divided ivory plate, sampled physical transfers and a route-specific preview; its plate uses the planned wood-physics prototype pending ceramic validation. No downloaded assets, brand artwork or third-party licences were introduced.

Reusable shapes/material helpers come from the project's existing blackjack_art.gd and track_builder.gd; breakfast scenes contain no casino props or card textures. Runtime surface patterns are generated from world coordinates. The saved environment and route preview are produced by the kitchen authoring recipe through Summer resource/scene APIs.
